About Us

Slice House has been slinging hand-tossed pizza since 2010. Every pie starts with dough proofed for 48 hours, topped with local ingredients, and baked in a real wood-fired oven until the crust gets that perfect char.


Whether you're grabbing a slice on your lunch break or ordering a full pie for family movie night, we've got you covered — fast, fresh, and always made with love.
